N.C. State Fair

The N.C. State Fair is the ultimate fall tradition in Raleigh! Every October, the fairgrounds come alive for 11 days of epic carnival rides, live music, cute farm animals, and tons of wild deep-fried treats (yes, even fried butter!). It's the biggest party in the state and the perfect place to hang out with family and friends. 🎡🍕

Fun things to do at the N.C. State Fair!

N.C. State Fair is packed with fun activities for everyone! From thrilling rides to delicious food, there's something for all ages. Don't miss the chance to experience the excitement and create unforgettable memories at this iconic event!

Here are some of the fun activities you can enjoy at the fair:

  • 🎢 Ride the thrilling roller coasters and Ferris wheel for an adrenaline rush!
  • 🐄 Visit the petting zoo and meet adorable farm animals up close.
  • 🎶 Enjoy live music performances from local bands and artists.
  • 🍔 Indulge in delicious fair food, from funnel cakes to fried Oreos!
  • 🎨 Explore arts and crafts exhibits showcasing local talent.
  • 🔥 Watch the spectacular fireworks display every evening!
